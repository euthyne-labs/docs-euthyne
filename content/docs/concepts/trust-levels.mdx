---
title: Trust levels
description: Signed, Witnessed and Full proof, and what each one means.
---

Every receipt has a trust level. It is a property of the receipt, reached step by step as proofs arrive.

| Level | Reached when | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed | The signature is valid, the key is bound to a human and an organization, and the key was not revoked at that point in the log | At hook time |
| Witnessed | The receipt's digest is included under a signed log checkpoint | Within seconds of upload |
| Full proof | Changes only. Witnessed, coverage is `covered`, and someone who is neither the author nor the launching human approved the final head | After merge and the timestamp |

## Signed

A key that belongs to a known device, bound to a known human and organization, signed this exact receipt. Nothing in it changed after signing.

Signed alone does not show when the receipt was made. The time inside comes from the device clock.

## Witnessed

The receipt is in Euthyne's append-only public log. From then on, removing or changing it would be detectable.

A receipt shows **Witnessed, timestamp pending** as soon as it is logged. A few hours later, the log checkpoint is timestamped independently through OpenTimestamps, and the timestamp is no longer pending. The anchor proves the receipt existed before that time, without relying on Euthyne.

## Full proof

Full proof applies to changes, not single actions. It means:

- the change receipt is Witnessed,
- every commit is accounted for by a linked session (coverage `covered`),
- and an approval on the final head came from someone other than the author and the launching human.

Full proof does not mean the code is good. It means the record is complete by Euthyne's rules, and someone else signed off on the forge.

## What never counts

[Detected signals](/docs/concepts/detected-signals) never count toward any level. They are unsigned.

For the exact checks behind each level, see [the four checks](/docs/spec/checks).
