---
title: euthyne reveal
description: Reveal one committed field to an auditor.
---

<Callout title="Coming soon">Planned command. Not released yet.</Callout>

```bash
euthyne reveal <receipt> <field>
```

Prints the salt and the value for one field of one receipt, for example `tool_input`. The auditor recomputes the commitment and checks it matches the receipt.

Revealing one field opens nothing else. It only works on the device that holds the commitment key, or with your organization's own copy of that key.

See [Commitments](/docs/spec/commitments).
